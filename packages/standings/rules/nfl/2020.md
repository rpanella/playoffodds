# NFL tie-breaking procedures — era `nfl@2020` (2020 season onward)

**Source:** NFL, "NFL Tiebreaking Procedures", https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-tiebreaking-procedures (also served at https://www.nfl.com/standings/tie-breaking-procedures). Page metadata: published 2019-04-23, modified 2025-02-18. Retrieved 2026-10-21.

**Why 2020:** the playoff field grew from 12 to 14 clubs in 2020 (seven per conference: four division winners, three wild cards, only the No. 1 seed gets a bye). The tie-breaking steps below are the current official text. If the league changes the procedure, add a new era module instead of editing this one.

Implemented in `src/leagues/nfl/2020.ts`.

## Official steps (condensed from the source; step order is exact)

Tie games count as one-half win and one-half loss for both clubs.

### Division tie

**Two clubs:** (1) head-to-head (best won-lost-tied percentage in games between the clubs); (2) best percentage in division games; (3) best percentage in common games; (4) best percentage in conference games; (5) strength of victory in all games; (6) strength of schedule in all games; (7) best combined ranking among conference teams in points scored and points allowed in all games; (8) best combined ranking among all teams in points scored and points allowed in all games; (9) best net points in common games; (10) best net points in all games; (11) best net touchdowns in all games; (12) coin toss.

**Three or more clubs:** the same twelve steps, with step 1 being the best percentage in games among the clubs. Official note: "If two clubs remain tied after one-or-more clubs are eliminated during any step, tiebreaker restarts at Step 1 of two-club format. If three clubs remain tied after a fourth club is eliminated during any step, tiebreaker restarts at Step 1 of three-club format."

### Wild-card tie

If the tied clubs are from the same division, apply the division tiebreaker. If they are from different divisions:

**Two clubs:** (1) head-to-head, if applicable; (2) best percentage in conference games; (3) best percentage in common games, minimum of four; (4) strength of victory; (5) strength of schedule; (6) combined ranking among conference teams (points scored and allowed); (7) combined ranking among all teams; (8) best net points in conference games; (9) best net points in all games; (10) best net touchdowns in all games; (11) coin toss.

**Three or more clubs:** (1) apply the division tiebreaker to eliminate all but the highest-ranked club in each division; "the original seeding within a division upon application of the division tiebreaker remains the same for all subsequent applications of the procedure"; (2) head-to-head sweep, "applicable only if one club has defeated each of the others or if one club has lost to each of the others"; (3)–(12) as two-club steps 2–11. Official note: two clubs remaining restart at step 1 of the two-club format; "if three clubs remain tied after a fourth club is eliminated during any step, tiebreaker restarts at Step 2 of three-club format."

After the first wild card is identified, the procedure is repeated for the second and third, again eliminating all but the highest-ranked club in each division first.

### Other procedures

1. "Only one club advances to the playoffs in any tie-breaking step. Remaining tied clubs revert to the first step of the applicable division or Wild Card tie-breakers." When one club wins, all other clubs revert to step 1 of the applicable two-club or three-club format.
2. Common-opponent records compare won-lost-tied percentage, since clubs may have played unequal numbers of games.
3. Home-field priority among division winners: wild-card tiebreakers.
4. Home-field priority among wild-card qualifiers: division tiebreakers if from the same division, otherwise wild-card tiebreakers.
5. Combined ranking: add the club's position in points scored and in points allowed; lowest total wins. Clubs tied for a position each get that position ("1, 1, 3").

## Seeding (2020 format)

Per conference: division winners are seeds 1–4, ordered by percentage with wild-card tiebreakers. The three best remaining clubs are seeds 5–7, ordered by percentage with the wild-card procedure. Seed 1 has a bye.

## Interpretations (not spelled out in the source)

These choices are ours. Each is covered by a fixture.

- **Restart after any reduction.** The source spells out 2 remaining (restart two-club) and 4→3 remaining. We apply the same rule to any reduction that leaves three or more clubs (for example 5→4): restart the multi-club procedure (division: step 1; wild card: step 2, since the division pruning has already been applied).
- **Strength of victory** is the combined percentage of the opponents a club defeated, counting each win separately (an opponent beaten twice counts twice). Tie games are not victories and are excluded.
- **Strength of schedule** is the combined percentage of all opponents played, each game counted separately.
- **"Combined percentage"** is total wins plus half of total ties over total games across the opponents, not an average of their percentages.
- **Common opponents** are teams every tied club has played. Each club's percentage covers all its games against those opponents. For wild cards, the step applies only if every tied club has at least four such games.
- **Head-to-head sweep** means the club beat each other tied club in every meeting (no losses or ties against any of them). A sweeping club wins the step. Otherwise, a club that lost every meeting with each other club is eliminated.
- **Zero games in a category.** If any tied club has no games in a percentage step's category (possible only mid-season, for example head-to-head before the clubs meet), the step does not apply and the procedure moves on.
- **Canceled games** (2022 BUF–CIN) are excluded. Percentages handle unequal games played.
- **Net touchdowns** are not available from SportsCrunch. The step reports itself unavailable: deterministic standings flag the tie as unresolved; simulations use a seeded draw and count it.
- **Simulated worlds have no scores.** Points-based steps (7–11 division, 6–10 wild card) use a seeded draw in any world containing a simulated game, and the draw is counted (D47).
