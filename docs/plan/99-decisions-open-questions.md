## Decisions D50–D52 (2026-10-21, after reviewing PoolCrunch)

| ID | Date | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D50 | 2026-10-21 | **PlayoffOdds computes clinch and elimination itself, at launch.**<br><br>**What:** the standings package gets a clinch module. For each team and target (playoff spot, division, top seed), a search over every possible remaining result returns clinched, eliminated or alive. "Controls its own destiny" comes from the same check.<br><br>**Why it can't be wrong:** a tie that would need an undecided tiebreaker is assumed to go against the team when proving a clinch, and in its favor when proving elimination. A computed status is never wrong; at worst it is later than the league's announcement.<br><br>**Official markers** (ESPN crawl via SportsCrunch) become a cross-check, and fill the gap when the league announces first.<br><br>**Timing:** NFL statuses ship at launch. This week's clinching scenarios are the first M4 item. MLB magic numbers come with MLB; NBA and NHL with their onboarding. The launch target moves to about Dec 16, before Week 15.<br><br>Supersedes D48; narrows D33 and D43. | The owner wants clinching, magic numbers and scenarios as PlayoffOdds capabilities, not a scraped label, and accepted a short launch delay for it. NFL clinches happen in Weeks 14–18, the launch window. A conservative check is sound and much cheaper than a full exact proof system; exact tiebreak handling can tighten it later |
| D51 | 2026-10-21 | **Drift comes from measured historical line movement (PoolCrunch).**<br><br>**What:** each simulated season shifts every game's odds with a per-team random walk on the log-odds scale. The walk uses the team component of PoolCrunch's fitted NFL line-drift generator (`poolcrunch/ml/simulator/params/line_drift_generator_v2.json`, fitted on 2014–2025 weekly boards from the SportsCrunch feed): variance by weeks ahead, Student-t tails with ν ≈ 4.79. PoolCrunch's per-game news, market-wide shift and final-week jump are not ported.<br><br>**Before the picker:** split the measured movement into a result-driven in-sim update plus a smaller walk, so a team that wins in a simulated world gets stronger later while total movement still matches the measurement.<br><br>**RNG and ties:** counter-based random streams keyed by (sim, purpose, entity), as in PoolCrunch. Tie rate is PoolCrunch's measured 0.373%.<br><br>**Other leagues** re-run the drift measurement on their line history before onboarding.<br><br>Replaces the Elo-style update in D47; narrows D6; completes D8 for NFL. | The owner's intent was to shift the odds based on historical benchmarks, and PoolCrunch already measured them on the same feed. The placeholder (Elo K=20) understated rating movement about 2.5×, which would have made the odds overconfident. Per-game and market-wide noise barely changes season outcomes, since it is independent from game to game. Keyed streams keep a pinned game from reshuffling every other draw, which makes the picker's changes against baseline much less noisy |
| D52 | 2026-10-21 | **Playoff games are priced from a SportsCrunch matchup-odds matrix, not team ratings.**<br><br>**What:** SportsCrunch adds `GET /seasons/{season}/matchup-odds?at=`, which runs its existing predictor on every ordered pair of teams (home matrix) plus neutral sites. The engine prices each simulated playoff game from the matrix entry, with the same drift as regular-season games. Until the endpoint exists, the M0 spike builds the matrix from a fit to remaining-game anchors, labeled `fit`.<br><br>**Later:** if SportsCrunch is rebuilt around one underlying rating per team, it produces the same matrix from those ratings, and PlayoffOdds doesn't change.<br><br>Narrows D14, D22 and D47. | SportsCrunch blends several predictors, so one rating per team can't reproduce its output without a rebuild. Running the existing predictor on every pairing is easy. A 32-team matrix is under 1,000 numbers, small enough to store in every snapshot input and ship to the picker |
